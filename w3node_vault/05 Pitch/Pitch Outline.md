# Pitch outline

5 minutes. 2–3 slides. Then 3 minutes of questions ([[Q&A Prep]]). Score against [[Judging Rubric]].

## 0:00 — Problem (about 60s)

Informal traders, spaza shops in South Africa as the concrete case, are unbanked. Services built for them cannot take a card. The same shops produce local data and are not paid for it. Cost, airtime, and a bad signal are the setting, not a footnote.

## 1:00 — HowlPay (about 45s)

A platform opens a USDT wallet for the shop. The shop pays for service credits. The platform pays the shop when data is verified. Phrase stays on the phone. Name is a working title.

## 1:45 — Show the payment (about 90s)

One screen: credits, rand, USDT, network. Approve or cancel. Point at the video if the live network is risky. Say the cash-out is simulated. If you only have the unfunded wallet, say the script refuses to sign rather than inventing a receipt.

## 3:15 — How it is built (about 60s)

Tether WDK. Sepolia. Five steps, then the same hash on reconnect. The script is the signer today. The phone UI does not pretend to sign. This is testnet, not a local mock of a chain.

## 4:15 — Why it can be a business (about 45s)

Platforms already want these shops. They need a way to get paid and a reason for the shop to open the app again. Mobile money is the cash-out, still simulated. Next is a community relief pool: pooled USDT, automatic payout on a verified incident.

Stop at 5:00.
