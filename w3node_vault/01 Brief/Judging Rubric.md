# Judging rubric

90 points. Ties go to impact and scalability.

## Product — 30

Polished, intuitive UX. A judge should follow a top-up from review to receipt without a narration rescue. The simulated cash-out has to look simulated.

## Technical — 30

Clean code, deployed on testnet or mainnet, core features working. A **local-only demo caps around 14**. Talking to Sepolia from the wallet script is the testnet evidence, and a live R20 top-up has now confirmed (see [[Testnet]]). The Expo shell is not a store build. Do not describe the phone UI as a deployed wallet.

## Business — 30

A specific African problem. Local context: cost, connectivity, and literacy. Integration with M-Pesa or mobile networks (ours is simulated and labelled). Commercial viability: a platform pays to offer credits, shops get a reason to keep the wallet because verified data pays them.
